Privacy Policy for Calorie Flow

Last updated: February 24, 2026

Calorie Flow is a 100% offline application. No user data is collected, stored, or transmitted from your device.

All information entered remains exclusively on your device

in a secure local database (Room/SQLite):

  • Profile details (age, gender, height, weight, activity level) – used only to calculate your calorie goal.
  • Logged meals and favorite foods – stored locally for your personal use.

External Connections

The only external connection is a direct request to the public USDA FoodData API for food search queries. No personal data is sent; only the food names you search for are transmitted to retrieve nutritional facts.

Data Security

  • No personal data is collected.
  • No data is sent to servers, clouds, or third parties.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs are used.
  • No access to location, device ID, IP address, or identifiers.

Your data never leaves your phone. No internet connection is required for the app to function.

Your Control

You can delete all your data at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings.
