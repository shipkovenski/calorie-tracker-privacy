Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 24, 2026

Calorie Tracker is a 100% offline application. We do not collect, store, or transmit any data from your device.

1. Data We Handle

We only store what you manually enter in the app:

  • Profile information (age, gender, height, weight, activity level) – used only to calculate your calorie goal
  • Logged meals and favorite foods (name, calories, macronutrients) – for your personal tracking

We do not collect or access:

  • Location, device ID, IP address, or any identifiers
  • Analytics, tracking, or advertising data
  • Any data from external APIs (no internet connection required)

2. Data Storage

All data remains exclusively on your device using local Room database. Nothing is sent anywhere.

3. Your Control

You can delete all data at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings.

4. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted here.
